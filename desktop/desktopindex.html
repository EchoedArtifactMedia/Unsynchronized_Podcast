About the Podcast


Transcript | Show Notes

One band. Five voices. So many questions. The breakup of *NSYNC is often treated like a footnote: inevitable, uncomplicated, already explained. Unsynchronized challenges that narrative. Through archival fragments and emotional realism, it asks what was lost, what was hidden, and what still remains unsaid. Find us on Spotify, Apple Podcasts, Amazon Music, YouTube Music, TuneIn, iHeart Radio, Castbox, RSS.com, and Pocket Casts.

About the Host

Nicole Raposo is the host and creator of Unsynchronized. A self-described “bootleg archivist,” she has spent years tracing the residue of *NSYNC’s breakup, documenting contradictions, silences, and the pieces left behind. Her findings were first shared through Riddle On Her Mind, a multi-year visual archive mapping the group’s quiet unraveling from 2001 to 2004. Building on that research, Unsynchronized presents the most thorough public exploration of the group’s final years, combining historical analysis, archival detail, and original synthesis. Raposo’s work represents a one-of-a-kind record of *NSYNC’s history, and she is widely recognized as the foremost research authority on the group’s breakup.
