Summary

In The Buzzing ’Skeeto, we walk through Chris Kirkpatrick’s 2001, examining how he balanced life as a member of *NSYNC with entrepreneurial ambitions beyond music. From the growth of FuMan Skeeto and media appearances to increasing discussion about the future of teen pop, Chris navigated a pivotal year marked by creativity, business development, and the challenges of sustaining relevance in a rapidly changing entertainment landscape.

Topics Covered:

  • Entrepreneurship and creative control
  • Balancing duty with expansion
  • Media pigeonholing
  • The "ticking clock" narrative for teen pop
