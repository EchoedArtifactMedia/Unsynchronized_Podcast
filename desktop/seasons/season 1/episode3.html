Summary

In The Buzzing ’Skeeto, we walk through Chris Kirkpatrick’s 2001 and how he navigated a year that was busier — and more complicated — than it looked from the outside. This episode follows Chris’ push for creative control through FuMan Skeeto, the day‑to‑day balancing act of running a fashion line while staying fully committed to *NSYNC, and the ticking clock of a pop landscape that was starting to age out its stars. Along the way, we look at the interviews, the media moments, and the small details that shaped how fans saw him — and how much they didn’t see at all.

Topics Covered:

  • Entrepreneurship and creative control
  • Balancing duty with expansion
  • Media pigeonholing
  • The “ticking clock” narrative for teen pop
