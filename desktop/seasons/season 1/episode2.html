Three Films and a Baby


Transcript
Published: 12.25.2025

Summary

In 2001, Joey Fatone balanced fatherhood, film work, and relentless group activity — from award shows and the Super Bowl halftime spectacle to recording and releasing Celebrity, while also launching the PopOdyssey Tour. Injuries, rumors, and solo press for On the Line revealed both his resilience and vulnerability, but throughout the year Joey remained deeply committed to *NSYNC’s milestones, showing up for every major event even as questions about the future quietly surfaced.

Topics Covered:

  • Joey’s visibility at the People’s Choice Awards and Super Bowl halftime show
  • The birth of Briahna Fatone alongside filming On the Line
  • Launch of the PopOdyssey Tour and release of Celebrity
  • Injury and recovery during the “Pop” video shoot
  • Solo press window and promotion for On the Line
  • Burnout admissions contrasted with charity and media appearances
  • Closing the year with holiday specials and the “Girlfriend” video premiere
