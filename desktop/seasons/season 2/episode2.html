Summary

No Small Parts is our look at 2002 through the eyes of Joey Fatone, a year in which he took his career in new directions at the start of *NSYNC's  break. Over the course of the year, we see Joey remain committed to his new projects in theater and film while staying connected to the group, revealing how he navigated a period of change with steadiness and focus.

Topics Covered:

  • Returning to technical roots through live theater
  • Character acting and ensemble work in independent film
  • Branching out while remaining loyal to *NSYNC
  • Tempered optimism during the early "hiatus"
