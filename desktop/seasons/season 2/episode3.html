Summary

In 2002, Chris Kirkpatrick approached *NSYNC's break not as a split, but as a temporary pause for creative evolution. Fraying Edges explores a year defined by steady philanthropic work, growing uncertainty surrounding FuMan Skeeto, and Chris's persistent drive to keep creating through setbacks and losses.

Topics Covered:

  • Keeping Busy During Downtime
  • Perception and Uncertainty Around FuMan Skeeto
  • Resilience and Persistent Creativity
